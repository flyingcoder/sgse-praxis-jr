# Decide

Follow the `praxisjr-design` skill for this decision only. Present viable options, answer the six change-control questions, delegate to the `praxisjr-reviewer` agent, and write a proposed decision record. Do not implement.

Arguments: the decision. Example: `/praxisjr-decide store invoices in the existing documents table`.
